RENOVATION GUIDELINES FOR PARCEL OWNERS
Issued by the Joint Management Body (JMB) | Effective 1 June 2025

1. APPROVAL
1.1 Owners must submit Form R1 with drawings at least 14 days before work starts.
1.2 A refundable renovation deposit of RM 3,000 is payable on approval.
1.3 Works during the 24-month Defect Liability Period (DLP) that alter wet areas, waterproofing or M&E void the
    developer's defect warranty for the affected area. Owners are advised to lodge DLP defects first.

2. WORKING HOURS
2.1 Monday to Friday 9.00 am to 5.00 pm; Saturday 9.00 am to 1.00 pm.
2.2 No works on Sundays or public holidays. Hacking is limited to 10.00 am to 12.00 noon on weekdays.

3. PROHIBITED WORKS
3.1 No structural changes to columns, beams, slabs or shear walls.
3.2 No relocation of wet areas (bathrooms, kitchens) without a PE-endorsed waterproofing design.
3.3 No changes to the building facade, including window frames, grilles colour and balcony enclosures.

4. DEBRIS AND ACCESS
4.1 Debris must be bagged and removed daily; use of the service lift only, with protective padding.
4.2 Contractors must register at the guardhouse and wear the issued pass.

5. PENALTIES
5.1 Breach of working hours: RM 200 per occurrence deducted from the deposit.
5.2 Unapproved structural works: stop-work order and forfeiture of the full deposit.
