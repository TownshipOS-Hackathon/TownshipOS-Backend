EMERGENCY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Property Management Office | Revision 5, February 2026

1. CLASSIFICATION
1.1 An emergency is any incident with immediate risk to life or of major property damage, including: person trapped
    in a lift, fire or smoke, burning smell or sparks from electrical equipment, gas smell, burst pipe or flooding,
    falling ceiling or facade elements, structural cracks that widen visibly, and intruders on the premises.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (first 15 minutes)
2.1 The duty officer calls the relevant emergency service where life is at risk: BOMBA 999, Police 999.
2.2 The duty officer notifies the Property Manager by phone, then the relevant contractor per its SLA.
2.3 For lift entrapment, the duty officer reassures the occupant via intercom every 5 minutes and does not attempt
    a manual release; only the lift contractor performs releases.
2.4 For electrical incidents, isolate the affected distribution board if safe to do so.

3. ESCALATION
3.1 If the contractor has not confirmed dispatch within 30 minutes, escalate to the contractor's account manager
    and log the time; this counts toward SLA penalties.
3.2 The Property Manager informs the JMB chairperson within 2 hours of any emergency.

4. RESIDENT COMMUNICATION
4.1 A holding message in Bahasa Malaysia and English is sent to affected residents within 30 minutes.
4.2 Do not speculate on causes or promise repair times before the contractor's assessment.

5. RECORDS
5.1 Every emergency is logged with time received, time contractor notified, time on site and time resolved.
5.2 An incident report is filed within 24 hours and tabled at the next JMB meeting.
