LIFT MAINTENANCE SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Township: Serenia Heights (demo) | Contractor: OTIS Malaysia | Revision 3, January 2026

1. SCOPE
1.1 This agreement covers all 16 passenger lifts in Blocks A to D.
1.2 Preventive maintenance is carried out every 3 months per lift, in accordance with DOSH requirements.

2. RESPONSE TIMES
2.1 Emergency (person trapped, lift free-fall, fire mode fault): technician on site within 1 hour, 24/7.
2.2 High (lift out of service, doors not closing, abnormal noise or vibration): technician on site within 4 hours.
2.3 Medium (indicator faults, cosmetic damage, intermittent issues): attended within 2 working days.
2.4 Low (cleaning, lighting inside car): attended at the next scheduled visit.

3. RESOLUTION TIMES
3.1 Emergency faults shall be resolved or the lift safely isolated within 4 hours of arrival.
3.2 High faults shall be resolved within 24 hours unless parts must be imported, in which case the contractor
    shall notify the Property Manager in writing within 24 hours with an estimated date.

4. PENALTIES
4.1 Failure to meet the 4-hour response time in clause 2.2 incurs a penalty of RM 500 per incident.
4.2 Failure to meet the 1-hour emergency response time in clause 2.1 incurs a penalty of RM 2,000 per incident
    and is reported to the JMB at the next meeting.
4.3 Penalties are deducted from the following month's invoice.
4.4 Three or more penalties in a rolling 6-month period entitle the JMB to terminate with 30 days notice.

5. REPORTING
5.1 The contractor submits a monthly report listing all call-outs, response times and parts replaced.
5.2 Lift certificates (PMA) must be renewed 30 days before expiry; copies kept at the management office.
